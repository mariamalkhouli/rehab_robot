Mimicry Mode — Teach & Replay
Before starting the session, please read the workflow below.
1
Attach the cuff
Press Attach Position to move the arm to a safe spread position, then attach the cuff to the patient's leg.
2
Jog the arm
Use the axis jog buttons (◀ ▶ per axis) to move the arm to each desired position. Each press moves 1°.
3
Save keyframes
Press Save Keyframe after each position. Build up a sequence of at least 2 keyframes to define the path.
4
Start Replay
Once your path is ready, press Start Replay. The robot will smoothly interpolate between your keyframes and repeat the path for the configured number of repetitions.
⚠️ Always keep one hand near the E-STOP button while jogging. Move slowly and verify patient comfort at each keyframe.
